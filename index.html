About Me

I am an AI engineer with 4+ years delivering systems that leave the notebook. The current focus is large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, and ChromaDB: quizzes from class notes, meeting minutes, lecture notes, and exam checking.

Documents are chunked, embedded, and stored in ChromaDB with metadata for the course, document, and date. The model answers from the passages retrieval returns. That pipeline sits behind SmartQuiz AI, the AI Meeting Assistant, Teacher Assistant, and E-checking on samnol.com.

Alongside that, I have worked on the same vision and field projects as the rest of the team: water-safety kits, plant growth, surveillance, traffic, agriculture, and edge deployment on Raspberry Pi and Jetson Nano. Eight papers cover language, surveillance, water, agriculture, and retail.
